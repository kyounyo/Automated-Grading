Melissa: 30 years old and weighs 64kg
Tony, Melissa’s husband: 32 years old and weighs 73kg
Bella, their first child: 12 years old, weighs 39kg
Bobby, their youngest child: 7 years old, weighs 23kg
Instructions: Using the information provided to you above, calculate how many chocolate squares each member of the family will require for their first dose.
